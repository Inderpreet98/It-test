🚀 Easy Step-by-Step Installation Guides

1. Plug in & Boot upInsert your Windows USB installer into the computer. Turn the PC on and keep tapping the boot key (usually F12 or F2) until a small menu pops up. Choose your USB drive to start.
2. Choose LanguageClick your language, country, and keyboard layout from the list, then click the big "Install Now" button.
3. Pick "Custom" InstallChoose "Custom: Install Windows only". This ensures a clean, fresh setup rather than an upgrade patch.
4. Select Storage DriveClick on your main hard drive partition from the list and hit Next. Windows will configure the space for you.
5. The Critical UnplugWhen files finish copying and the system says it is restarting, pull out the USB drive so the setup loop doesn't restart.
1. Boot into Live DesktopPlug in your Linux USB drive, start the machine, and choose "Try or Install Ubuntu". This runs a working test sandbox without changing your drive yet.
2. Open Setup WizardClick the desktop shortcut icon named "Install Ubuntu" to activate the installer window.
3. Drive Layout AllocationsSelect "Erase disk and install Ubuntu". This automatically wipes any old data and configures the standard Linux partition settings.
4. Account ProfilesPick your time zone map coordinate and write down your username, computer network label, and a private login password.
1. Enter Recovery ModeTurn off your Mac. Hold down Cmd + R (Intel Mac) or hold the physical Power button (Apple Silicon Mac) until recovery options display.
2. Disk Utility FormatOpen "Disk Utility", click on your main storage drive container, choose "Erase", format it cleanly as "APFS", and close the tool.
3. Reinstall macOSSelect "Reinstall macOS" from the utility grid layout menu, click continue, choose your drive, and stream the setup files from the network cloud.
